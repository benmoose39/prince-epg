11:30am - Come Dine with Me
On day two of the competition in Worcestershire beauty pageant queen and dinner party virgin Pippa Langhorne throws a sophisticated `Midsummer Night's Dream' evening for her guests. Pippa's fishy starter doesn't get a great response from shellfish-hater Claire or oddball Tim, who claims fish are his friends. The main goes down well with foodie Teresa, who thinks it very flavoursome, but not so well with moaner Chris.
