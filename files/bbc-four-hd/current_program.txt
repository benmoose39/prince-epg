6:30pm - Wainwright's Walks
Julia Bradbury faces more climbing than walking as she sets out on one of the most dramatic Lakeland routes of all. From the remote valley of Ennerdale, she starts the ascent to one of Wainwright's favourite fells Pillar. Taking in everything the mountain has to offer, Julia's route takes her past cliffs, along ledges and over the Lake District's most famous crag, Pillar Rock.
