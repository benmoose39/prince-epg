6:30pm - Wainwright's Walks
Julia Bradbury is in heart of the Lake District in the village of Grasmere for a climb up one of Alfred Wainwright's smallest but favourite fells. Helm Crag is defined by the collection of rock formations at its summit a feature that has lent it the nickname, the Lion and the Lamb. The rocks make for a summit scramble for Julia as she finds out why this was the only summit Wainwright himself never truly reached.
