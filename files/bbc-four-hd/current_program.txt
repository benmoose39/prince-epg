8:15pm - Pam St Clement Remembers... EastEnders: Pat and Frank's Affair
Pam St Clement was an instant hit with EastEnders' audiences from the moment she first appeared on Albert Square, playing the tough - but occasionally tender - Pat Butcher. Here, Pam looks back on one of her biggest moments, remembering how the nation was gripped by one the soap's most iconic episodes, when Pat's affair with Frank was finally, and very publicly, exposed.
