6:30pm - Timothy Spall: Back at Sea
Timothy and Shane take their barge to three different countries and the Isle of Man. From Whitehaven, where Timothy learns about the pirate John Paul Jones, they steam over to Douglas to visit his son, actor Rafe Spall, who is there to work on BBC2's `The Shadow Line'. Next, they visit a city Tim loves dearly, Belfast, and a special pub he says is the finest drinking establishment in the English-speaking world.
