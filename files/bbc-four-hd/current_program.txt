11:20pm - Welsh Greats
From Swansea Council house boy to beloved singer, actor, comedian and presenter, Sir Harry ruled the airwaves with shows ranging from the `Goon Show' and `Pickwick to Highway' and `Songs of Praise'. With the help of Peter Sellers and Spike Milligan, the programme illustrates his infectious humour and deep-felt faith, and reminds us of his tragic battle with a stroke and cancer.
