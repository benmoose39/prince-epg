6:30pm - Wainwright's Walks
The beautiful outline of Catbells is familiar to all who visit Derwent Water and the Lakeland town of Keswick. By boat and by foot, Julia sets out to discover the secrets of a classic fell-climb for all the family. However, famous views mask a story that is rich in history - miners trod where visitors walk today, when Catbells produced minerals more valuable than gold.
