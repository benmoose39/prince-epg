9:20pm - 	Fanny and Alexander
The story of two children belonging to a wealthy, extensive theatrical family in provincial Sweden in the early years of the 20th century, 10-year-old Alexander and his younger sister, Fanny. When their father dies unexpectedly during a performance and their mother decides to remarry, the children are forced to relocate to the austere home of their stern and rather coldhearted stepfather, Bishop Vergerus.
