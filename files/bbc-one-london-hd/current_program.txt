12:15pm - Bargain Hunt
Today's Bargain Hunt comes from Kingston upon Thames. Christina steps back in time to the stylish 1930's when she visits the sleek art deco Lido in Saltdean. The teams, alongside their experts, John Cameron and Richard Madley, hope to make a splash when they take their three items to the auction in Cranbrook, where auctioneer Raj Bisram will be wielding his gavel.
