2:45pm - Garden Rescue
Flo and Charlie are in Twickenham to transform Micheal and Jill's unloved and flowerless patch and derelict filled-in pond into a Hawaiian paradise, complete with tiki bar. They want a place to socialise, with a grand dance floor patio to boogie the night away with the local troop! Can the team achieve all of this on a £7,000 budget?
