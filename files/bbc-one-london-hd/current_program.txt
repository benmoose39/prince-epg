10:30pm - Match of the Day
A busy Premier League Sunday, with five matches, including Liverpool against a Brighton side who've started the season strongly, and Manchester United at home to struggling Bournemouth. Elsewhere, another early season form side, Brentford, travel to Hull City, two of last season's promoted clubs, Sunderland and Leeds United, meet at the Stadium of Light, and Pierre Sage's Crystal Palace take on Newcastle United.
