8:00pm - DIY SOS
Nick and the team travel to Kidderminster to help a family whose lives revolve around caring for their son Nathan. The 11-year-old can have up to 200 seizures a day and needs round-the-clock care, leaving parents John and Emma exhausted. Meanwhile, teenage sister Kira has seen more and more of her home taken over by Nathan's needs. With the help of hundreds of volunteers, the team creates a home that keeps Nathan safe while giving the whole family the space to live together again.
