11:10pm - The Celebrity Inner Circle
Amanda Holden hosts this exciting quiz show, packed with strategy, high stakes and lots of surprises. Five contestants choose a celebrity partner and are assigned a secret stash of cash, worth anything from zero to a whopping £6,000. The twist? Each pair only knows their own value before being thrown into a battle of wits, nerve and strategic gameplay across a series of challenging rounds. Their mission? Uncover who has got what amount, outsmart their rivals and secure their spot in the final.
