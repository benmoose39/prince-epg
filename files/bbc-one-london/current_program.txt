11:15am - Bargain Hunt
Find out who an intriguing collection of collectibles belongs to when Christina Trevanion chats to a close friend and colleague from the show. There's even an appearance by his dog! In Malvern, Worcestershire, two teams shop for bargains at the Three Counties Showground. In the red corner, it's friends Joy and Adetola, guided by David Harper. In the blue corner, it's couple Michelle and Michael, with expert Danny Sebastian.
