9:40pm - Stacey Dooley
Rehab centres are treating some of their youngest-ever and most physically damaged addicts, and ketamine has been their drug of choice. Stacey Dooley gains rare access to a rehab in Stockport and gets to know former users. Many are in chronic pain, and one young man needs bladder reconstruction surgery. Over four months, she follows them through recovery, relationships, setbacks and celebrations.
