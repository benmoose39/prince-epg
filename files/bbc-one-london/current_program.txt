4:40pm - Countryfile
Matt Baker and Emily McGowen are in the Cotswolds for the biggest date in the sheepdog trialling calendar - the Supreme. The finest dogs and handlers from across the UK and Ireland go head-to-head in a thrilling showcase of instinct, skill and control. After months of training and a year of tough qualifying trials, it all comes down to this - but only one will claim the coveted title of Supreme Champion.
