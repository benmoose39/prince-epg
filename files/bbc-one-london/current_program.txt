2:00pm - Escape to the Country
North Yorkshire is calling, and Briony May Williams is on the case as she helps a London buyer and her beloved dog Buddy swap city bustle for a country cottage. With a £350,000 budget as well as her human best buddy by her side for support, the trio discover charming homes, towns and villages for this rural getaway. Briony also meets a family living on the edge of the amazing moors, where they now roast their own coffee beans for their thriving cafe.
