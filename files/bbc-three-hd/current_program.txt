9:00pm - Stacey Dooley
In 2021, 19-year-old soldier Jaysley Beck was found dead in her army barracks. Through unprecedented access to her family, friends and former colleagues, Stacey Dooley traces her journey from excited young recruit to victim of sexual assault. As her family seeks answers and fights for change, the film explores the experiences of women in the armed forces and asks what more can be done to protect them.
