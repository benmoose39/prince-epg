8:00pm - Stacey Dooley Sleeps Over
Autumn picked up her first gun at the age of two. Now, at just nine years old, she has 36 guns of her own and a dedicated YouTube channel where she reviews and tests out semi-automatic weapons for her 200,000 subscribers. Dad Randy and mum Lindsay live their life on the road, moving from state to state in their RV. They only settle in states with relaxed gun laws so that they can practise Autumn's passion unhindered.
