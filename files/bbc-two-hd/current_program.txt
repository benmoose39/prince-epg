6:00pm - House of Games
Every day this week, celebrity contestants Maisie Adam, Ade Edmondson, Rickie Haywood-Williams and Sammi Kinghorn take on a series of quiz rounds selected by the host, Michael Sheen. A daily winner is declared following a quickfire round at the end of each show, and the scores are tallied across the week, resulting in an overall champion being crowned on Friday. Will the winning stars opt for the much-coveted House of Games decanter, or will they be tempted with the House of Games record player?
