5:30am - Escape to the Country
Denise Nurse turns up the heat in south Devon as she helps Susi and Sam, along with Rupert the dog, exchange their busy Bedfordshire town for life in the West Country. With £500,000 to spend, an annexe for visiting friends and family is a must, but a few glorious sea views could prove rather distracting. Things get hotter still when Denise meets local chilli farmers and puts her taste buds to the test with some of their fiery goodies.
