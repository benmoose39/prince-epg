5:20am - Bargain Hunt
Indulge in a splash of seaside nostalgia with host Caroine Hawley and two more teams of antiques hunters. In Bristol, Caroline visits the Martin Parr Foundation to view a genre-defining exhibition by the late photographer Martin Parr CBE. It's 40 years since Parr produced his iconic collection of images of working-class family holidays in the Merseyside resort of New Brighton.
