8:30am - Joanna Page's Wild Life
Actor Joanna Page continues her lifelong dream of training to be a wildlife rescue volunteer as she helps to triage and rehabilitate a fledgling little owl who has been brought into the wildlife rescue centre. Joanna also rescues a hedgehog from a local garden. A worried resident has seen it being rolled across a lawn by his hedgehog mate. Happily, it's a story with a romantic ending.
