8:30am - Joanna Page's Wild Life
A hedgehog comes into the wildlife rescue centre with a nasty wound on its head. Vet Judith treats it with Joanna Page as her able assistant as she trains to be a volunteer. Joanna loves feeding an owl who has been brought in. She's less keen on the food prep that has to happen first, though. Two tiny swift chicks come in needing specialist care. And Joanna gets to release a now-healthy hedgehog.
