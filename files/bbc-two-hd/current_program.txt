8:50pm - Natural World
From the mighty grizzly bear to the endearing real life Paddington, the spectacled bear, and Rudyard Kipling's Jungle Book's Baloo, the sloth bear, this remarkable animal family has long captured our imagination. As some of the largest animals on earth, they need more than just the `bare necessities' to survive especially in today's ever-changing world. This film explores how bears across the world have overcome the challenges of life, from finding food to raising the cubs.
