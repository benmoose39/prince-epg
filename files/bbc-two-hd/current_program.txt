6:45am - Bargain Hunt
In this special episode, four well-known Radio DJs go head-to-head at one of Britain's biggest antiques fairs to raise money for charity. Hoping to spin loads of profits are BBC Radio 2's OJ Borg and his teammate Melvin Odoom opposite BBC Asian Network's Nikita Kanda and Radio 1's Maia Beth. Let's hope their antiques experts Izzie Balmer and Nick Hall are on the same wavelength as they hunt for bargains among the stalls. A brass pipe sparks a conversation about foreign languages for the reds.
