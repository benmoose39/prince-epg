5:00pm - Richard Osman's House of Games
Richard Osman takes to the host seat for a final week of quizzing before he hands over the baton to a new host next week. He is joined by previous winners Miles Jupp, Cariad Lloyd, Lucy Porter and Kiell Smith-Bynoe, who go head-to-head, testing their general knowledge skills in a variety of fun trivia-based games. A daily winner is declared following a quickfire round at the end of each show, and the scores are tallied across the week, resulting in an overall champion being crowned on Friday.
