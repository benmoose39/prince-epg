5:30am - Bargain Hunt
It is youth versus experience as a quirky red team shop against the more senior blues at the Bath and West Showground near Shepton Mallet. A large pair of cattle horns and some Dalton cauldrons are among the items up for auction in Bristol. Guiding the teams are antique experts Jeremy Lamond and Henry Meadows. Presenter Tim Wonnacott takes time out to visit William Morris's country residence in the Cotswolds.
