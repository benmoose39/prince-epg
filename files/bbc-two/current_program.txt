5:00pm - BMW PGA Championship, DP World Tour Golf
Action from the final day at Wentworth as a world-class field contests the flagship event on the DP World Tour. The winner at the iconic Surrey venue adds their name to an illustrious list of previous champions, including Rory McIlroy, Luke Donald, Seve Ballesteros and Nick Faldo. Two-time winner Alex Noren defends the title he won in 2025 after a dramatic play-off victory last year.
