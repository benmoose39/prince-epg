5:30am - Escape to the Country
Jules Hudson is in the Kent countryside with Londoners Rob and Dani, who are hunting for a new home for them, their son Oscar and Bonnie the dog. Jules mixes it up with homes old and new before turning things upside down with the mystery house. And there's plenty more growing in the Garden of England when Jules ventures inside a staggering 51-acre greenhouse filled with tomatoes and peppers.
