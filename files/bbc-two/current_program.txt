7:30am - Joanna Page's Wild Life
A beautiful peregrine falcon fledgling with a very noisy screech is brought into the rescue centre - a chance for a nervous Joanna Page to learn about the behaviours and care of a bird of prey. Joanna also learns how working with wildlife can be hard and rewarding in equal measure and how important wildlife is. She amazes her four young children with a video of her scruffing a tiny wood mouse.
