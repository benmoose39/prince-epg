6:10am - Bargain Hunt
While the teams get into the rhythm of their shopping, host Danny Sebastian indulges one of his passions at an exhibition in London. The Music Is Black: A British Story celebrates 125 years of the influence of black music on culture and communities. At the V&A East Museum, Danny tours the exhibition with curator Jacqueline Springer, looking at a vinyl record with the words 'Race Records' on the sleeve, a label designed to segregate black music from white artists.
