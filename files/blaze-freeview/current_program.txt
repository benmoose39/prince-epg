7:00pm - Dickinson's Real Deal
David Dickinson and his dealers are in Ilkeston in Derbyshire where a lady in her nineties charms Tony Geering, but will he buy her 1920s motorbike helmet? A lady brings in a wartime snuffbox made by her father for Alison Chapman, but today's star is auctioneer Annabelle Brammer with her inside knowledge of rare local pottery.
