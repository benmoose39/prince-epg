8:35am - Deadly 60
Steve descends deep into the Waitomo caves to find an unusual predator that lures insects to their death with a lethal lightshow. Then he heads to South island, to get an aerial view of one of the biggest animals on Earth; before driving inland to meet a very curious bird armed with deadly intelligence. Finally, Steve returns to the ocean in search of sperm whales and uses his kayak to get an awe-inspiring close-up view.
