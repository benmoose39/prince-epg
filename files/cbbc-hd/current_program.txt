2:30pm - My Life
Jazmine is determined to raise awareness about her little sister Amaya's rare chromosome condition, which is so uncommon that it doesn't even have a name, but is known by doctors as 1Q43Q44 Deletion Syndrome. Amaya can't walk or talk, but the bond between the two sisters is evident. In this heartwarming film, nine-year-old Jazmine welcomes us into her family home to meet her siblings and see the chaos of daily family life.
