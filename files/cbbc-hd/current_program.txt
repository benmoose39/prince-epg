11:15am - Go Jetters
Tala decides to focus a class project on her uncle Glitch's souvenir selfies from Europe - but little does she know that all of his pictures are atrocious. A panicking Glitch gets help from the Go Jetters, and using a series of cardboard cut-outs they start to take photos all across the continent - but can they help out when Glitch gets himself into a sticky situation?
