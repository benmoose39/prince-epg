11:15am - Go Jetters
Ubercorn and Glitch head to the Svalbard Global Seed Vault, hoping to keep some seeds from Glitch's rare fruit tree safe for the future. But, just like when they were cadets together, the two of them get into trouble. Glitch accidentally crashes the Grimbler into the vault, and its hot engines start melting the ice that keeps the seeds cool. With the Go Jetters delayed by a malfunctioning Jet Pad, Ubercorn and Glitch try to save the scientists, the vault and Glitch's rare seeds.
