2:30pm - Operation Ouch!
Dr Chris and Dr Xand find out what happens when you eat with the help of a miniature Xand doll. They test your ears and eyes in a baffling mind-bending trick, and Dr Chris is at work fighting infectious diseases. Meanwhile there's an unusual 'itis' in the ouch-mobile, and over in accident and emergency one patient has cut their head and another has a very wonky finger.
