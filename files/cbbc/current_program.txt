11:30am - Hey You What If?
Telekinesis, the power to be able to move things with the mind, might be popular in films but it's not possible in reality. While watching his favourite show about a villain who can lift anything with mind-power, our hero finds out that he can do it, too. And not only that, he finds out that the villain in the show is real when he goes outside to put the rubbish out. Before long the two are pitched in a mind-power battle in the alley by the recycling bins.
