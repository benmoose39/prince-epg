10:45am - Blow Your Mind: Spectacular Space
Dr Chris and Dr Xand van Tulleken learn all about space. They find out all about the length of our days and how they are getting longer - and that it's all because of the moon. Their friend, space scientist Dr Maggie, discovers some clever coral that can tell the time and their speedy chum Kate Humble drives a car at over a thousand miles an hour - amazing!
