4:30pm - Treasure Champs
Barry doesn't know what to say when Kari reminisces about her grandma. But he finds some of Grandma's old belongings for Kari to enjoy and they celebrate by throwing a party in her memory, feasting on some of Grandma's famous Super Soup. We meet some Treasure Champs who visit an air raid shelter museum to find out what life was like in the past, and another who buries a time capsule with her school class to help children in the future discover what life is like now.
