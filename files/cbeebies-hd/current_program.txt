9:25am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Remi, George, Zadie, Idris, Felicity, Tommy and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags in this episode are cardboard boxes which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate cardboard boxes inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby! The fun song `The Wheels on the Bus!' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear.
