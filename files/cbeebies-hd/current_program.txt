9:20am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Amber, Amelia, Arthur, Ezra, Luke, Willow, Xander and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bag in this episode are hats which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding an appropriate hat inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby! The nursery rhyme 'Dingle Dangle Scarecrow' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear.
