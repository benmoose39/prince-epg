5:30pm - JoJo & Gran Gran
JoJo and Gran Gran visit the community garden to pull up the winter vegetables. They get some help harvesting the broccoli and cauliflower from their friends Ellie and Alfie, but it's not time to dig up the carrots because they won't be ready until the spring. However, that doesn't stop the birds from trying to eat them! Alfie scares them off, but he won't be able to be there all the time. Gran Gran comes up with a plan - they can make a scarecrow to keep the birds away.
