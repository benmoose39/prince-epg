9:25am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Connor, Bonnie, Ian, Dhilan, Addi, Gracie and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags are wooden spoons which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate wooden spoons inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby! The popular nursery rhyme `Little Miss Muffet' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear.
