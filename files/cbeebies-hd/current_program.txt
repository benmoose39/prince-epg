9:35am - My First
After seeing a funny clown in school, Isabella is inspired to put on her own circus at home. After trying some juggling and hula-hooping, she soon realises that she could use a little more practice, so she and her mum decide to head over to a real circus to learn from the best. Once they arrive, they are amazed at the performers of the show, and Isabella even gets picked to hula-hoop with a clown. Once the show is over, they both head back home to get dressed up in their new costumes.
