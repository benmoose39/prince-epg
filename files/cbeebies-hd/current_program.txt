9:25am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Olive, Jack, Arabella, Leo, Emma, Jonas and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags in this episode are socks which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate socks inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby! The fun song `Head, Shoulders, Knees and Toes' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear.
