9:40am - My First
After spending time playing in the garden and taking pictures, Dylan decides that he would like to learn how to use a camera properly. He and his mum head over to visit a camera museum, where Dylan is shown more about cameras and even taught how to make his own pinhole camera. Once they've learnt the basics, they head over to the local park to take pictures with an instant camera so that they can print the images out straight away.
