3:35pm - Treasure Champs
Kari has been invited to Barry's house for a playdate, but he becomes possessive over his toys and won't share anything with her. Realising the error of his ways, he then goes too far the other way and gives Kari everything, making her feel rather overwhelmed. It's only when Kari creates a den with some of Barry's toys and welcomes him in that he learns the value of hospitality.
