9:20am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Marta, Ferne, Inam, Isaac, Luther, Hawa and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Parents, carers and their babies at home are invited to join in with all the fun activities too! Inside everyone's bags are porridge oats which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding safe, baby appropriate porridge oats inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal.
