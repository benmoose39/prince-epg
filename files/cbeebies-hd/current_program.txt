10:45am - Our Family
Kyra and Khloe play at being a fairytale queen and princess, but when Khloe eats a pretend magic apple, she falls into a deep sleep. Later, they enjoy a walk in the park with Mum, and there is more pretend play, with leaf monsters and crocodiles. Back at home, Kyra and Khloe become chefs and help Mum make a special dinner for the whole family.
