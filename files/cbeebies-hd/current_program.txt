9:20am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Layla, Betsy, Tobias, Amelia, Amber, Ferne, Ezra and their grown-ups. Inside everyone's bags are sticks which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding safe, baby appropriate sticks inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby.
