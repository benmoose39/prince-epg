9:20am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Ferne, Roman, Rosa-May, Sehaj, Benjamin, Elias, Isobel and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Parents, carers and their babies at home are invited to join in with all the fun activities too! Inside everyone's bags are mirrors which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding safe, baby appropriate mirrors inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal.
