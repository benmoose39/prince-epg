9:20am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Evie, Gracie, Luke, Hunter, Thea, Benjamin and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags are different types of toy transport which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding safe, baby appropriate toy vehicles inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby!
