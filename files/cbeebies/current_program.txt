9:25am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Isobel, Idris, Lincoln, Amber, Amelia, Jason, Willow and their grown-ups. Inside everyone's bags are pans which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate pans inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby.
