1:35pm - Treasure Champs
Kari is convinced that the 'no ball games' sign doesn't apply to her, but she learns her lesson when she accidentally hurts a flower. Barry teaches her that rules should be obeyed and are always there for a reason, so she goes to play with her ball in a different area, putting up her own signs to ensure there are no elephants, seagulls or giant fish - and no flowers.
