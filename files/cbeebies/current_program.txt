11:35am - Go Wild
Aneeshwar and Lizzie head to a safari park to meet a herd of zebras. They reveal that a zebra's stripes are unique to each individual. Not one zebra looks the same. Next, Aneeshwar and Lizzie encounter some red-bellied piranhas. These fish have huge teeth, and they see a large shoal of piranhas right in front of them being fed their breakfast. Finally, Aneeshwar and Lizzie discover what the wild connection is between these two incredible animals.
