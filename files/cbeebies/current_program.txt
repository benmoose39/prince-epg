9:20am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Noah, Baani, Millana, Amber, Amelia, Jace, Hunter and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags are cups which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate cups inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby!
