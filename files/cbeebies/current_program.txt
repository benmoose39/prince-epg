9:40am - My First
During a picnic with his family, Max and his family discover an amazing lighthouse with lots of stairs. After having lots of fun climbing all the way to the very top, his parents ask him if he would like to try the next challenge: rock climbing! The family all head over to a rock climbing wall. It's very high, but Max isn't afraid and after just a few lessons feels like he's ready to climb to the top all by himself. After a successful climb, Max then decides it's time for his last big challenge.
