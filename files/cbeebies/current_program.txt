9:20am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Marta, Hud, Theo, Isobel, Sehaj, Isaac and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Parents, carers and their babies at home are invited to join in with all the fun activities too. Inside everyone's bags in are teddy bears which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate teddy bears inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby.
