9:25am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Amber, Amelia, Arthur, DJ, Zadie, Betsy, Indianna and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags are leaves which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate leaves inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby. The song `All the leaves are falling down' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear.
