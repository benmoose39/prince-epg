9:25am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Remi, Otis, George, Holly, DJ, Luke and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags are cardboard tubes which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate cardboard tubes inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby. The popular song `Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear.
