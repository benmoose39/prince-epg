11:20am - Biff and Chip
When strange things start to happen at the Pages' house, Wilf and Chip become convinced that Mrs Page is actually an alien in disguise. The boys are excited by the idea of being the first people to meet this real-life alien visitor, so they pack some gifts and set about trying to make friends with it. They even try speaking in alien language so it will understand them! Will the boys make friends with the alien and get to fly in its spaceship after all?
