4:35pm - JoJo & Gran Gran
JoJo and Gran Gran spot a bird's nest in Cynthia's garden, which inspires JoJo to make a nest for Panda. Cynthia and Gran Gran teach JoJo about all the nests that different birds make to help their babies. Gran Gran has a plan: while JoJo waits for the eggs they found to hatch, they can go to the park and see all the ducklings that have already hatched out and about! Meanwhile, Daddy helps teach JoJo how to whistle just like a bird.
