9:40am - My First
After playing in the garden with her bubbles, Maisie's mum explains that it's time to get ready for her first day of nursery. Maisie's new teachers arrive at her home to explain to her what she will be doing at nursery and what to expect. After travelling to the nursery, her mum stays with her for the first visit to help her adjust to the new environment. Once the day finishes, the two of them head over to get Maisie's uniform and school shoes ready for the start of nursery.
