9:20am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Roman, Rose-may, Eliza, Theo, Joseph, George, Gracie and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags are oranges which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate oranges inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby!
