9:20am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Jean, Elijah, Noah, Baani, Marta, Evie, and their grown-ups to The Baby Club for a special episode featuring dads and male carers. Inside everyone's bags are beach toys which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate beach toys inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby!
