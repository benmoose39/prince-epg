4:40pm - JoJo & Gran Gran
When Mummy sets off for work at the hospital, JoJo wants to know what a job is. To explain, Gran Gran takes JoJo to see their friends, who work in all their different jobs. While they're out, they realise Mummy accidentally took Gran Gran's phone to the hospital with her. Now JoJo has a job, visiting Mummy to fix the mix-up and making sure she gets her phone back.
