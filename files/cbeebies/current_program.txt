9:40am - My First
Whilst drawing a picture of a fish at home, Jaxon's dad has a great idea and asks Jaxon if he wants to learn how to fish for real. They head out and visit a fishing shop that sells whatever fishing supplies they will need for their exciting trip. They begin by visiting a fishing lake to try out their new fishing rod. Soon after, Jaxon and his mum travel to a fishing aquarium to learn about all the different kinds of fish. They finish the day by travelling out to sea on a boat.
