9:40am - My First
While helping his family shop for groceries, Sam decides he would like to go fruit picking with his mum for the first time. After the family arrive at the fruit farm, they pick some strawberries, while Sam meets a real life farmer who shows him around the inside of his tractor. When Sam gets home, he decides to plant a strawberry plant in his garden so that one day, when it grows, he will be able to pick his own fruit at home.
