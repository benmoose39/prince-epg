9:25am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Idris, Tobias, Holly, Jack, Dhilan, Isobel and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags in this episode are sponges which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding baby appropriate sponges inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby! The baby song `We use a sponge to wash!' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear.
