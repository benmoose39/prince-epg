9:20am - The Baby Club
Giovanna and Baby Bear welcome babies Joseph, Baani, Jean, Amber, Amelia, Luke and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Parents, carers and their babies at home are invited to join in with all the fun activities too! Inside everyone's bags are dollies which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. Grown-ups at home are encouraged to take part by hiding a safe, baby appropriate dolly inside a fabric bag or pillowcase for them to reveal and explore with their baby!
