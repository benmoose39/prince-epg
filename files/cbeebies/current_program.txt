9:25am - The Baby Club
Nigel and Baby Bear welcome babies Tommy, Remi, Felicity, Otis, George, Zadie and their grown-ups to The Baby Club. Inside everyone's bags in this episode are ribbons which are explored through discovery, play, song and story. The popular baby song `Wind the bobbin up' features, as does a story involving Baby Bear. Plus, the Action Song gets everyone moving about before some calm time with bubbles and the Goodbye Song.
