7:30am - Fireman Sam
Today is the grand opening of the Pontypandy mountain railway line, but the station isn't ready as Gareth has been too busy refurbishing the old engine, The Pontypandy Flyer. With all the fuss over the steam engine, Elvis worries that Jupiter will feel left out, but when Sarah and James accidentally cause a fire at the water tower while decorating the new station, it is Jupiter that saves the day.
