5:30pm - Nadiya's Family Favourites
Nadiya Hussain makes a smokey spinach shakshuka, a squid risotto, a twist on Sunday lunch and an orange and coffee poke cake. Nadiya's quest to find the best homemade marmalade in Britain takes her to Preston, where she meets 102-year-old Nora, and her penchant for a good cuppa lands her on Britain's first ever tea plantation in Cornwall.
