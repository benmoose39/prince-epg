10:30pm - Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives
This trip, Guy's taking Kid Rock's lead on a tour through D-town. Just outside of Detroit in Clarkston, Michigan, Guy tries one of Kid Rock's favourites, mac 'n cheese, in a renovated church. On the same block, Guy and Kid Rock check out a neighbourhood favourite scratch making bacon and hot links for one mammoth sandwich. In Webberville, Michigan, Kid Rock shows Guy the secret behind his own unique brew.
