9:30pm - Mary Berry's Foolproof Cooking
Mary showcases her dishes that are perfect for a party. Sometimes cooking for a crowd can be very daunting, but Mary loves sharing her ideas that are fun, different and don't take forever to prepare. First up is a Thai chicken curry, perfect for a party and all people need is a fork and a bowl! Mary loves a classic dish, and what better way to showcase this than with her ham hock terrine.
