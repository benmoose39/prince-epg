6:30pm - Mary Berry's Quick Cooking
Mary is off to join the Navy onboard one of its most powerful warships, the HMS Defender. She learns how they turn out hundreds of meals a day against the clock and on a tight budget. Whilst the crew run a war training exercise on board the ship, Mary heads to the galley to see if she can help the kitchen team with the mammoth task of prepping, cooking, and serving 240 meals, from scratch, in just 75 minutes.
