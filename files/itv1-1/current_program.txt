3:00pm - 
Jill Douglas presents live coverage of the Nations Championship as Scotland face next year's Rugby World Cup hosts, Australia at Murrayfield. It's been two years since these sides last faced each other and Scotland came out on top, beating the Wallabies 27-13 at Murrayfield. Scotland captain Sione Tuipulotu scored the first try against the country of his birth. Three second-half tries followed as Gregor Townsend's side secured their fourth win from their last five matches against Australia.
