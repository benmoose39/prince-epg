8:30pm - Bradley and Barney Walsh: Breaking Dad
The boys are back! Bradley and Barney Walsh embark on a 3,000-mile journey through Europe, starting off in the sunny south of Croatia and ending up in the ice-cold Arctic Circle in Scandinavia. There's no easing his dad in on this trip for Barney. First up, they swing off the roof of Split's iconic football stadium, before diving headfirst into the crystal clear waters of the Adriatic Sea. The action continues when the pair go head to head on aqua bikes alongside a former world champion.
