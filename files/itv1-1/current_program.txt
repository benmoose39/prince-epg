6:00am - Fletchers' Family Farm
It's a busy time on the farm as the family prepares pigs for the butcher, ready to become sausages for their stall at the Royal Cheshire Show. But Liz's plans are interrupted when she discovers an injured hen that has been badly pecked by the cockerel. She takes the bird to her friend Debbie, a chicken expert, who gives it some much-needed care before it returns to the farm sporting a rather stylish accessory.
