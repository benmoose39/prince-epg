5:55am - Fletchers' Family Farm
On her morning rounds, Liz takes a moment to appreciate the life the family have built on the farm, but their peaceful routine is interrupted when calf Elizabeth develops a worrying wheeze. Fearing pneumonia, the illness that claimed beloved Cherry last year, Kelvin and Liz call in vet Hugo to investigate. Meanwhile, with the pigs gone, the family begin transforming the old pig field into a vegetable patch.
