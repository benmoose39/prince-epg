11:30am - Fletchers' Family Farm
As the fifth series starts, we join the Fletchers adjusting to life in their lodge and living back on their land. Plans are in for the farmhouse rebuild following last year's fire, and as they await the green light, there's plenty to keep them busy. Kelvin takes time to reflect on how far they've come since buying the 120 acres five years ago, and shares their exciting plans for the year ahead.
