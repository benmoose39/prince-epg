7:30pm - It'll Be Alright on the Night
Comedian Tom Allen unveils backstage bloopers and live blunders featuring the world's finest TV shows and stars - expect unseen outtakes from our favourite dramas, comedies and entertainment shows. Starring Alexander Armstrong, Richard Ayoade, Rob Beckett, Ant and Dec, Alan Carr, Patrick Kielty, Coleen Nolan, the cast of Heartstopper and many more, the cock-ups this time include disaster striking on Britain's Got Talent when Alesha Dixon experiences an unexpected wardrobe malfunction.
