8:30pm - Cooking with the Stars
Emma Willis and Tom Allen host as four stars - Joey Essex, JB Gill, Caroline Quentin and Harry Akines-Aryeetey - take on each other in the first-ever four-way cooking battle. They'll have to produce stand-out dishes from a recipe they've never seen before in just 30 minutes. The two stars who impress the most go straight through to the quarter-finals, and the losing stars will have to impress the world-class chefs and cook again in the cook-off - where one will leave the competition.
