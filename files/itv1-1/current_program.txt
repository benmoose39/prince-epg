9:55am - Cooking with the Stars
Emma Willis and Tom Allen host as three new contestants - reality star Joey Essex, pop star and presenter JB Gill and actor Lesley Joseph - go head-to-head in a three-way cooking battle. They'll have to produce delicious BBQ dishes using indoor grills. The star with the best dish goes straight through to the next round, while the other two will have to face the cook-off - where one star will leave the competition.
