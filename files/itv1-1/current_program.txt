12:45pm - 
Ed Chamberlin presents coverage of live racing from Ascot, Newmarket and Redcar. Ascot stages a competitive afternoon of racing including the Cumberland Lodge Stakes over a mile and a half, whilst the Group One racing this afternoon comes from Newmarket where the Sun Chariot takes centre stage, plus there's two races from Redcar. Analysis from Jason Weaver and Adele Mulrennan, reporting from Rishi Persad and Natalie Green with commentary from Richard Hoiles.
