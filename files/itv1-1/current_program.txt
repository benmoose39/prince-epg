8:00pm - 
England are back in action in the Nations League, and take on Spain at the Wanda Metropolitano, the home of Atletico Madrid. The two teams met back in September where the World Champions ran out 3-2 winners at Wembley. Since then England have played well including a 7-0 away win against Croatia and are now in line for a top two finish in the group stage, and are on track for the knockout rounds of the Nations League in March.
