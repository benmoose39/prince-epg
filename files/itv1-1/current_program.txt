7:30pm - It'll Be Alright on the Night
Comedian Tom Allen uncovers hilarious cock-ups from the sets of the world's greatest TV shows featuring our favourite stars - expect unseen outtakes from our favourite dramas, comedies and entertainment shows alongside on-air mishaps from the world of news, daytime TV and sport. While caught up in the chaos are Ryan Gosling, Stephen Fry, Damian Lewis, Alison Hammond, Alan Titchmarsh, Amanda Holden and many more, the cock-ups include an alpaca using the This Morning studio floor as a toilet.
