8:30am - Love Your Weekend with Alan Titchmarsh
Joining Alan Titchmarsh at Manor Farm this Remembrance Sunday are racing hero Billy Monger and Gavin and Stacey's Laura Aikman. Plus, a celebration of the role of donkeys during the world wars, and the D-Day Darlings perform a Vera Lynn classic. Then, original emergency service vehicles used to help wounded soldiers during the war years make their way onto the show.
