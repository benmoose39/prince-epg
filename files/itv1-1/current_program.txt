Lauren Salter is joined by guests Dan Biggar and Sam Warburton for live rugby from round four of the Nations Championship as Wales host Japan at the Principality Stadium. Wales are looking to make a winning start against Eddie Jones' Japan side, who they beat by a single point in Cardiff a year ago. Every point will be crucial ahead of two tough fixtures against New Zealand and Australia, which will close out the Northern Series for Wales.
