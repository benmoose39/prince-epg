2:00pm - 
England continue their autumn campaign in the Nations Championship with a massive test of their credentials against New Zealand at the Allianz Stadium. The two sides last met a year ago when England were in irresistible form as they surged to a 33-19 victory. After a poor Six Nations, another win against Dave Rennie's new look All Blacks would be massive confidence boost for Steve Borthwick and his charges. Mark Pougatch is joined by Ugo Monye, Sean Fitzpatrick and Danny Care for live coverage.
