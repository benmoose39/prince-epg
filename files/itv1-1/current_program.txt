7:00am - Fletchers' Family Farm
Agronomist Ben arrives to check on the farm's oats and maize, but before getting down to business, Max has an important call to make to Spider-Man. Once his superhero duties are complete, attention turns to the crops and how they are progressing. Meanwhile, Liz and Kelvin enjoy an unusual day date with a visit to local beekeepers. Hoping to bring bees to the farm, they discover what it takes to care for a colony and learn there is plenty to consider before taking the plunge.
