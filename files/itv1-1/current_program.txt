8:15pm - 
Jill Douglas presents live coverage of the Nations Championship as France face South Africa at the Stade de France in Paris. Last year, the Springboks left the Stade de France with a famous win, playing the second half with 14 men after Lood de Jager was sent off late in the first half. Rassie Erasmus' side responded by scoring three second half tries to come from behind and beat Les Bleus.
