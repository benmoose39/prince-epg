10:30am - Love Your Weekend with Alan Titchmarsh
Alan is back and is this time joined on the farm by comedy couple Paul Merton and Suki Webster along with presenter and adventurer Ben Fogle - we also meet a rare breed shorthorn cow, Jonathan Moseley shows off some sustainable spring floristry, and Tom Surgey brings a delicious selection of cheese and wine pairings to the barn in the Best of British feature.
