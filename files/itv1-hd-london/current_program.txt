Jill Douglas presents live coverage of the Nations Championship as Scotland face New Zealand at Murrayfield. Scotland have never beaten New Zealand, it's a run that goes back 121 years and stretches 33 matches. Last year, Gregor Townsend's side recovered from 17-0 down at half time to draw level with the All Blacks but a late try from Damian McKenzie helped maintain New Zealand's long unbeaten run against Scotland.
