6:00pm - 
Mark Pougatch is joined by Roy Keane and Ian Wright for live coverage of England's Nations League match against the Czech Republic in Prague. The teams last met five years ago in the group stages of the European Championship at Wembley, when an early goal from Raheem Sterling secured a 1-0 win for Gareth Southgate's side. After a disappointing end to the World Cup campaign, Thomas Tuchel will be looking to make a strong start in the Nations League.
