The violent murder of 18-year-old Henry Nowak shocked Britain after footage of his final moments came to light at the trial of his attacker in June. Captured on the police's body-worn camera, false accusations by the aggressor against Henry, alongside his pleas to police as he lay dying, caused national uproar. In an exclusive and deeply revealing interview with ITV's Paul Brand, Henry's family share memories of a much-loved son and brother whose life was cut tragically short.
