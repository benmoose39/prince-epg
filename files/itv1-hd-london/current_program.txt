4:20pm - 
Ireland continue their autumn campaign in the Nations Championship with a crunch match against the World Champions South Africa in Dublin. The two sides last met a year ago when the Springboks produced a scrummaging masterclass to earn a first win at the Aviva Stadium in thirteen years. Andy Farrell has admitted his Irish side are a work in progress and will need his experienced men like Tadhg Furlong, Andrew Porter and Jamison Gibson-Park to stand up and be counted.
