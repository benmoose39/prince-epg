5:30am - Love Your Weekend with Alan Titchmarsh
Joining Alan Titchmarsh at Manor Farm are Nigel Havers, Ken Bruce and Lesley Joseph. Plus, it's the launch of the Manor Farm puppy school, and horticulturalist Camilla Bassett-Smith and carpenter Wayne Perrey talk garden protection following the drought. Harvest festival tipples are the order of the day in today's Best of British, plus a celebration of 75 years of The Archers with some of the cast.
