7:30pm - 
Ireland continue their autumn campaign in the Nations Championship with a date against Fiji at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin. Andy Farrell's side picked up wins against Australia and Japan in the summer fixtures in the tournament and will be targeting another victory against a Fijian Side who have just appointed the South African Jake White as their new coach. Mark Pougatch is joined by Ugo Monye and Maggie Alphonsi for live coverage, with pitchside reports from Claire Thomas.
