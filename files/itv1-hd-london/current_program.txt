You may have seen it or even heard it - cars racing at speeds well over 100 miles per hour on public roads. This is street racing, also known as car cruising, when organised groups meet up to race, do tricks and even just spectate - however, these events can come at a huge cost with drivers and sometimes spectators getting injured and even being killed. Lewis Warner heads out into the night with car cruisers in the West Midlands playing cat and mouse with police forces.
