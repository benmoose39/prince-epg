Ed Chamberlin presents live coverage from Cheltenham, Doncaster and Newbury. Cheltenham stages its first Saturday meeting of the new Jumps season with plenty of Irish contenders trying to get on the scoreboard at the track early in proceedings, whilst there is also plenty of top-class flat racing to enjoy from Newbury and Doncaster, where the final Group 1 of the UK season takes place - the Futurity Stakes for the two-year-olds.
