2:00pm - 
England resume their Nations Championship campaign as they entertain Australia at the Allianz Stadium, Twickenham. After suffering a chastening defeat at the hands of South Africa in Johannesburg in July, Steve Borthwick's side chalked up a comfortable win over Fiji and a slender one over Argentina and now face an improving Australian side. The Wallabies pulled off a sensational 42-38 win over South Africa in Perth back in September.
