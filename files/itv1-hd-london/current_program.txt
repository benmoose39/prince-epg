10:45pm - It'll Be Alright on the Night
Tom Allen celebrates TV chaos with behind-the-scenes cock-ups and broadcast bloopers featuring the nation's favourite TV shows and stars - expect unseen outtakes from TV's finest dramas, comedies and entertainment programmes alongside on-air mishaps from the world of news, daytime TV and sport. Featuring Pedro Pascal, Jeremy Clarkson, Mel Giedroyc, Martin Lewis, Stephen Mulhern, James Martin, Prue Leith and more, the cock-ups include a live football interview being ruined by a rogue sprinkler.
