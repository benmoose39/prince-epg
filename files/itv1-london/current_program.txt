After launching in the summer the inaugural Nations Championship returns with the Northern Hemisphere leg of the tournament, which culminates with Finals weekend at the Allianz Stadium, Twickenham at the end of the month. After wins over Australia and Japan and a loss to New Zealand in July, Andy Farrell's Ireland will be aiming to start their autumn campaign on the front foot as they entertain Argentina at the Aviva Stadium.
