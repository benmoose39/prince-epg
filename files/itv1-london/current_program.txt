Mark Pougatch is joined by Roy Keane and Ian Wright for live coverage of England's Nations League match against the Czech Republic at Wembley. It's been a busy start to the autumn for Thomas Tuchel's side as they play their fourth match in just ten days. With some big names like Declan Rice, Marcus Rashford and Cole Palmer withdrawing from the squad it's an opportunity for Rio Ngumoha and the recalled Morgan Gibbs-White and Trent Alexander-Arnold to stake their claim.
