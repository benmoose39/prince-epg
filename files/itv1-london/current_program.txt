8:00pm - Winter
A river fisherman makes a gruesome discovery when human remains are found floating in the water. As Winter joins Bell and Daldry at the scene, a shocking revelation changes everything: the recovered limbs belong not to one victim, but two. With no complete bodies and few clues to follow, the investigation leads the team into a disturbing world of buried family secrets, online abuse and a tragedy whose devastating consequences continue to echo through the present.
