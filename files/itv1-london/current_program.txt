Francesca Cumani and Ed Chamberlin present coverage of the 2026 Prix de L'Arc de Triomphe from Paris-Longchamp. One of the biggest middle distance races of the season, the top stars from Europe take on some strong Japanese contenders in a race that always provides a superb spectacle. Analysis and thoughts from Rishi Persad and Adele Mulrennan plus commentary from Richard Hoiles.
