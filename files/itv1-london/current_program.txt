11:45am - 
Francesca Cumani and Ed Chamberlin present seven races at Ascot on Champions Day. The final day of the UK flat season will see young jockey Billy Loughnane crowned Champion jockey in amongst a superb day of racing with plenty of top-level action including the feature Queen Elizabeth II Stakes for the milers and the Champion Stakes itself. Analysis from Jason Weaver, Ruby Walsh and Adele Mulrennan, reporting from Matt Chapman, Luke Harvey and Oli Bell, and commentary from Richard Hoiles.
