The Carabao Cup fourth round has thrown up a classic tie between Liverpool and Chelsea. These two teams have met in big games many times over the years, including the final of this competition three times; Chelsea won the 2005 final and Liverpool won it in 2022 and 2024. This will be a special night for Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso who spent 5 years as a player at Liverpool, and he and the Liverpool manager Andoni Iraola know each other well, as they grew up playing football together in Spain.
