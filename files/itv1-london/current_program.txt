The Lionesses still need to qualify for the World Cup in Brazil next summer. They were in a tricky qualifying group with Spain, and finished second meaning they now have to go through a potential four playoff matches to qualify. First up they take on Greece, and they play away in Crete in this key match, if they beat Greece over two legs they will play either Slovakia or Ukraine to secure their place at the show piece event next summer.
