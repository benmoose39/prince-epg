4:30pm - 
Eddie Jones returns to the Allianz Stadium as his Japan side take on England in the Nations Championship. Jones guided England to a World Cup final and three Six Nations titles during his seven year spell in charge, but will now pit his wits against Steve Borthwick who was part of his coaching set up during this time. Borthwick's England team are still a work in progress - but will be aiming for a repeat performance of their last outing against Japan in November 2024.
