11:30am - Cooking with the Stars
Emma Willis and Tom Allen host as three new stars - Happy Mondays band member Bez, TV personality Scarlett Moffat and presenter Zara McDermott - and their mentors go head-to-head in a 3-way cooking battle. They'll have to produce gourmet dishes with a budget of just £3 a portion. If they fail to impress with their culinary creations, two of the stars will have to face the cook-off - where one star will leave the competition.
