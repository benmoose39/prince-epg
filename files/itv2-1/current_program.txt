6:00am - Dinner Date
Stephanie from Leeds is given five mouth-watering menus, each put together by a potential blind date. The five men behind the menus have all come up with a three course meal in the hope of tempting her into a date, but Stephanie will only be having dinner with three of them. She makes her choices based on the menus she most likes the look of, but when the three dates are over, who will she want to see for seconds?
