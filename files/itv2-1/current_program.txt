10:15am - The Only Way Is Essex
The unthinkable happens in Essex this week, as a rift tears through Girl Band, and everyone's loyalty is called into question. After Amy tells Saff that Harry believes Elma is the one spreading gossip back to Essex, Saff wastes no time passing it on. Elma confronts Amy and finally learns the root of Harry's anger, but the revelations don't stop there. Unbeknownst to Elma and Courtney, Amber has told Harry they've been talking about him in their private WhatsApp group.
