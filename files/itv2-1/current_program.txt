7:20am - Scooby-Doo! Mystery Incorporated
A sleepy little village, Crystal Cove boasts a long history of ghost sightings, poltergeists, demon possessions, phantoms and other paranormal occurrences. The renowned sleuthing team of Fred, Daphne, Velma, Shaggy and Scooby Doo prove all of this simply is not real and, along the way, uncover a larger mystery that will change everything.
