6:20am - Scooby-Doo! Mystery Incorporated
Shaggy and Scooby win a dinner date with their hero, Vincent Van Ghoul. What they do not realise is that they are unwitting participants in Van Ghoul's new reality television show where he has dinner with people, then scares them out of their wits. When a monster known as Nightfright locks the three up and terrorises them, it is up to Fred, Daphne, and Velma to solve the mystery and rescue their friends.
