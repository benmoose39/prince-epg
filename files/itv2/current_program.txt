6:00pm - Ant and Dec's Limitless Win
Ant and Dec return with their never-ending money ladder and a limitless cash prize - first up are husband-and-wife team Ricky and Manisha, who set their sights on a six-figure sum to fund a dream family holiday and some ambitious house renovations. Manisha has been compiling her very own Limitless Win spreadsheet packed full of facts and figures, and now she and Ricky try to translate all that homework into serious cash by winning truly life-changing money and leaving a legacy for their kids.
