After picking through an accumulation of school busses and a burned down storage house, they come across an item the guys have never seen before, place bets on what it actually is, and get surprised by what the appraiser says about it. Plus, Frank goes into picking-turbo-mode when he finds a gold mine of signs on a 20 acre farm.
