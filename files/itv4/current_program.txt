9:20am - Underwater Grand Fishing Adventure
Ali Hamidi is joined by Bobby Zamora and diver Luc Boerrigter as they return to The Lake of Dreams, aka Grenville. Having failed to land one of the lake's giants in front of the underwater cameras on their previous trip here together, this time they target a huge carp of over 60lbs. Being spring, the carp are constantly on the move around the lake, with the team in hot pursuit. Will the carp run rings around the team, or will the mobile tactics pay off to bank that big one?
