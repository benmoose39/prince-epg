9:30am - The Royal Procession: Celebrating 200 Years
To celebrate the 200th anniversary of the Royal Procession at Ascot, this documentary charts the history and heritage of the famed opening to the Royal Meeting - Alice Loxton explores why this tradition has stood the test of time since 1825 and remains so popular and important to this day, and we also hear from those who have experienced being in the Royal Procession firsthand and showcase some never-before-seen footage of key Royal Ascot moments.
