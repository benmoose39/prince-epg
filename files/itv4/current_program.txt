4:25pm - British Touring Car Championship
Highlights of the final weekend of the 2026 British Touring Car Championship season from Brand Hatch GP. In the previous meeting at Silverstone, Ashley Sutton clinched the championship, amassing an unassailable lead to become the first ever five-time BTCC champion. Other titles were still to be decided in the final three races on the Brands Hatch GP circuit. Nicki Shields presents the best of the action from Rounds 28, 29 and 30, with race commentary from David Addison and Tim Harvey.
