6:00am - British Touring Car Championship
Highlights of the ninth weekend of the 2026 British Touring Car Championship season from Silverstone's iconic circuit. In the penultimate weekend of the season, Ashley Sutton had the chance to win the championship and be the first man to win 5 BTCC titles. Nicki Shields presents the best of the action from rounds 25, 26 and 27, with race commentary coming from David Addison and Tim Harvey.
