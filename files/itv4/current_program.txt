2:20pm - Underwater Grand Fishing Adventure
Ali Hamidi is joined by angling buddy Roo Abbott and diver Luc Boerrigter at Millhayes Lakes in Devon. The mission is to catch a 30lb UK carp, with the bite filmed on an underwater camera. Small lakes in springtime can be notoriously tricky, so with the addition of a diver and underwater cameras, this will be no easy task. After catching some smaller specimens, they crack the tactics and bait code, but will they bank that illusive 30lber before the end of the trip?
