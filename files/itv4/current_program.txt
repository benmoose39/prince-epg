7:25am - American Pickers
While picking in Missouri, the guys stop in at Sam and Stan's steel yard where the father-son duo takes them on a high-speed golf cart tour of their massive hangar and outbuildings, loaded with heaps of metal and rusty junk, but can Mike and Frank uncover anything worth buying? The team also goes freestyling and comes across an old farm where the owners are about to have an auction and badly need Frank and Mike's help.
