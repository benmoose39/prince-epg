4:15pm - Four in a Bed
The first to host are husband-and-wife team Paul Billington and Flora Loizou from Silverwood Lodge in Cambridgeshire, the only couple in this week's competition. As the hosts show their three female guests to their rooms, Caroline, owner of The Dean Bed & Breakfast, gives a frosty response to the fact that they don't live on site, while Sue from Seashells Guesthouse is left in hysterics.
