4:15pm - Four in a Bed
In Spain, four sets of British expats battle it out to be named best value B&B. At Vista Del Torcal in the Andalucian Mountains, twenty-somethings Ben Eyre and Anna Chalu are the first hosts. While guests are impressed by their rooms, some are less enthusiastic about an afternoon of rock climbing. In the morning, Ben and Anna hope a full English breakfast can save the day after nighttime drama for Julia.
