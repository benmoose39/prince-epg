4:15pm - Four in a Bed
The new competition starts in Alderley Edge, Cheshire, where hosts Ian Lambert and his partner Ali Imiolek hope to cast a spell over the competition at their Wizards Thatch. The guests are Andrea Hickmott and Nick Bonner-Williams, lively well-to-do duo Dominic and Stephanie Parker, and James Reay and his partner Simon Dooley. Ian and Ali's continental breakfast basket is a bit too DIY for some.
