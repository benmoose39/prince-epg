6:20pm - Four in a Bed
The rivals reconvene for one last time to find out how much they've been paid by their fellow B&B owners, and to sort out their differences. Simon White of Old Hall Country House is stunned by a generous overpayment, but Eileen Finn from the four-star Thornley is on the receiving end of some harsh criticism and some even harsher payments, and a shock underpayment leaves Tim from The Cavendish in Bournemouth reeling.
