3:35pm - Henry VIII's Enforcer: The Rise and Fall of Thomas Cromwell
Thomas Cromwell has a reputation as one of the most notorious thugs ever to hold power in England. Oxford University's Professor Diarmaid MacCulloch, reveals another side to Henry VIII's Chief Minister. Cromwell emerges as a pioneering and principled statesman. He ignited a religious and parliamentary revolution, which laid the foundations for the modern British state.
