8:00pm - The Repair Shop
Jay Blades and the team bring four treasured family heirlooms, and the memories they hold, back to life. The first item to arrive is a double challenge for metal man Dominic Chinea and upholstery expert Sonnaz Nooranvary. Father and daughter Joe and Sophie Davies have a piece of their family trade's history that they are hoping to revive to be used again. Joe's father, Bill, opened a barber's shop in 1949, and both Joe and Sophie have followed in his footsteps.
