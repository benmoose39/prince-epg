3:30pm - 	Heartbreakers
Max and Page Conners are a mother-and-daughter team working the sweetest of scams. Mum marries a rich guy, Page gets him into a compromising position, Mum walks in and - hey presto - big divorce settlement. Until they try and latch onto tobacco tycoon William B Tensy, for whom Max actually begins to care, while Page falls for beach bar-owner Jack Withrowe, whose property happens to be worth a cool $3 million.
